Located near <mark style="background:#40a9ff">the **equator**</mark>, this region has a typical **tropical** climate. Every summer, the **monsoon** brings heavy rains, making the air extremely **humid**. This warm and wet environment is perfect for the **rainforest** to thrive. Because of this climate, you can find all kinds of delicious **tropical fruits** here.
