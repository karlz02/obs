### 思路断点1：找不到矩阵平方=0的相关反例

> [!answer]
> $A^2 = O \nRightarrow A = O$  
> 反例：$A = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{array} \right] \implies A^2 = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{array} \right]$
