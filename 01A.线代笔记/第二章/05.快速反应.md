快速反应1 向量乘法

> [!answer]
> * **行向量**：
>
> $$\alpha^T \implies \text{行向量} \implies \boxed{\phantom{\rule{24pt}{5pt}}}$$

> [!answer-joined]
> * **列向量**：
>
> $$\alpha \implies \text{列向量} \implies \boxed{\phantom{\rule{5pt}{24pt}}}$$

> [!answer-joined]
> * **行 $\times$ 列（内积 $\to$ 数）**：
>
> $$\alpha^T \alpha \implies \text{一行} \times \text{一列} \implies \boxed{\phantom{\rule{24pt}{5pt}}}\, \boxed{\phantom{\rule{5pt}{24pt}}} \implies \text{数}$$

> [!answer-joined]
> * **列 $\times$ 行（外积 $\to$ 矩阵）**：
>
> $$\alpha \alpha^T \implies \text{一列} \times \text{一行} \implies \boxed{\phantom{\rule{5pt}{24pt}}}\, \boxed{\phantom{\rule{24pt}{5pt}}} \implies \text{矩阵}$$

> [!answer-joined]
> -  补充：行列之间加个矩阵，三者相乘
> $$\alpha^T A \alpha \implies \underset{1 \times n}{\boxed{\phantom{\rule{24pt}{5pt}}}}\ \underset{n \times n}{\boxed{\phantom{\rule{16pt}{16pt}}}}\ \underset{n \times 1}{\boxed{\phantom{\rule{5pt}{24pt}}}} \implies \boxed{\phantom{\rule{24pt}{5pt}}}\, \boxed{\phantom{\rule{5pt}{24pt}}} \implies \text{数}$$
