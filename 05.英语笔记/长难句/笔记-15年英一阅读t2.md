### 长难句1

正如常见的情形那样,仅仅确立那项原则并不能降低具体划清界限的难度

- As so often is the case 这种情况屡见不鲜/正如往常一样
	- 固定句型用法 用来引出一种普遍存在的规律
- stating that principle 阐明那项原则
	- 动名词短语作主语
- ease 减轻
- line-drawing 具体划清界限

### 长难句2
 In many cases, it would not be <mark style="background:#fff88f">overly burdensome</mark> for authorities to obtain a warrant to <mark style="background:#fff88f">search through</mark> phone contents.
 在许多情况下,对于执法部来说,获取搜查令来搜查受控内容不会过于繁重
 - it would not be overly burdensome 不会过于困难
	 - overly 过度地
	 - burdensome 累赘的/繁重的
- search through 搜查

### 长难句3
The court, though, may want to allow room for police to cite situations where they are entitled to more freedom.
不过,法院可能希望为警察留出空间,允许他们举出自己有资格获得更多自由的情形
- though 不过
- allow room for 为...留有余地
- be entitled to 有资格获得

compares <mark style="background:#fff88f">the explosion and accessibility of digital information in the 21st century</mark> with <mark style="background:#fff88f">the establishment of automobile use as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th</mark>

	- accessibility 普及性/可获取性
- the establishment of automobile use as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th 20世纪汽车作为一种实质实质必需品的确立
	- establishment 确立
